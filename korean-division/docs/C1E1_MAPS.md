### Railway zone (b_annex) test values (coordinates TC-G will read)
- Rectangle x=792 y=264 w=216 h=192 (tiles [792,1008) x [264,456)). Separated from the urban area `b_city` by a grass strip 24 cells away, overlapping no other area. No rails (R9 alternative) — grid roads + house blocks + a boundary line of dirt road around the perimeter (from 1 cell outside the boundary to 1 cell inside).
- `--check` tests connectivity·separation (verification above). MapFile loading is confirmed with `--map c1e1_map_b --shot`.

### Terrain choices (vs the spec draft, can be modified by the user's oral statement)
- Urban area: house blocks placed irregularly by random numbers (fixed seed) + 2 cross avenues (houses are impassable so the avenues guarantee connectivity). Timber yard = rubble (the engine has no timber terrain).
- Dock: straighten the riverside (pier sand protrusion + warehouse houses) and the urban central road leads to the dock.
- Outer trench: north trench (y~414) + west trench (x~342) L-shaped, barbed wire (impassable) front row. The road is cut only at the road blockade point (`b_road_block`) → frontal breakthrough point. Can be bypassed around the ends of the barbed wire (east end of the north line x=720, south end of the west line y=864).
- 진강산: independent hill in the northwest (high circle radius 84), 4 field gun pits + observation trench (trench), 2 rocks. Battery unit placement is the mission JSON's job.
- Railway zone location = northeast of the urban area, opposite the dock on the east side of the river (the spec gives no location → arbitrary; question in DECISIONS_PENDING).
- The position·shape of the SK1 bypass route (`b_flank_path`) is also not in the spec, so it is assumed to be northward along the west edge + eastward toward the northern plain (question registered).
