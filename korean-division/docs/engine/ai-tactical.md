# Engine notes - ai-tactical (tactical AI stages 1~3B2, river crossing · counterattack · smoke, T24 fixes) (엔진 노트 - ai-tactical (전술 AI 1~3B2 단계, 도하·역습·연막, T24 수정안)) [split from ENGINE_NOTES.md 2026-10-23, body verbatim]

### Tactical AI stage 1 (전술 AI 1단계) (2026-10-19, ROADMAP ⑤, `src/AI/Tactical/` - `TacticalAi.cs` · `TacticalState.cs` · `AiProfile.cs`, test `Tests/TacticalSelfTest.cs` = `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=tactical`)
- **Default AI**: `AiRegistry.Default = "tactical"` - the default for standard games · `--battle` · `--quick-battle` · menu engagement/quick engagement · `BattleSetup.AiA/AiB`. `--ai battle|standard|passive|rush` remain for comparison (if `--ai` is given, the engagement test uses that AI too). `--ai tactical:<profile>` = fixed profile (`AiRegistry.Create` · `IsKnown`, name round trip -> snapshot OK).
- **3 layers** (the World calls `Tick` for each faction every 15 ticks -> call number `(Tick + f·7)/15`): commander = every 8 calls (≈2 s), battalion = `(call + i) % 4` (≈1 s), company = `(call + i) % 2` (≈0.5 s). Every call `VisibleEnemies` (Fog.CanSee) -> 8-tile grid `TacGrid` (nearest · radius statistics · live machine guns · cavalry target tiles).
- **Organization** (first call): rifle infantry with no company -> `FormCompany` of about 50 men in order of adjacent position (officers distributed evenly), company Id confirmed on the next call (`Resolve`). Existing companies (starting forces · map) are by majority branch of their composition. The rest = detachments (`TacKind`: Fire machine gun · anti-tank · direct-fire gun · sniper ≤8, Artillery indirect ≤6, Cavalry ≤30, Armor ≤6, Wagon ≤2, Scout ≤6). Rifle companies in fours form AI-internal battalions (`TacBattalion`, separate from world battalions); fire · armor · wagons go to the battalion with the nearest adjacent position, cavalry · artillery · scouts are directly under the commander.
- **Commander**: posture = mission directive > map `factions[].aiPosture` > profile posture. Attack objective = enemy group seen within the last 60 s (centered on the 40 tiles around the enemy closest to the front) > capture objective (a captured facility that is not ours · allied, a visible enemy city first) > enemy start position. If there is no weapon that can break armor, tanks are excluded from targets. Defense = from the start position (or area) facing the enemy, if no enemy is seen for `defendPatience` seconds (when there is no area to guard) it advances until contact. Hold area = counterattacks only enemies that come within area radius+15. Battalion front width = sum of front-line company widths, reduced to match the map width · (while attacking) enemy front width ×1.15+12, left-right order preserved. 3 or more battalions -> reserve battalion (committed on collapse · front under 70% · contact 40−20×aggressiveness s · 2× superiority). Battalion strength < retreat threshold ×0.75 -> retreats 20 s then reserve. If the front is empty, reserve committed · retreated battalion recommitted. `tactic: flank` = the outer battalion on the flank side obliquely.
- **Battalion**: 3 or more companies -> reserve company (rearmost), committed when a front company collapses · is under 75% · contact is long or even (visible enemies ≥ front strength ×0.8). Front companies = front zone in left-right order (`TaskPoint` · `TaskWidth` -> FormSpec Line + Dir + Width = same as a right-click-drag flag line). Fire = evenly 4 tiles behind the battalion position, armor = battalion position, wagon = 9 tiles behind the front company center.
- **Company**: before contact = attack move (flag line). Enemy within range+14 = attack-move to a firing position at engagement distance (`engageFrac` × range) (`BestCover`: within 4 tiles around, `TerrainCover` forest · rubble · trench · beside a house (plus shell crater terrain when it arrives) + high ground, barbed wire penalized, enemy side penalized) -> hold (stop and fire). Fire-line advance (bound) = after firing 14−10×aggressiveness seconds, if our suppression < 0.4 and favorable (average suppression of enemies within 12 tiles of the target ≥ 0.3 or we are 1.3×), a few tiles by a move command (march fire). Bayonet charge = distance ≤ chargeRange · our suppression ≤ 0.5 · no live machine gun within 10 tiles of the target · (enemy average suppression ≥ chargeSuppression or chargeable strength ≥ chargeOdds × enemy) -> point charge (`Charge`), 10 s · kept while charging. Defense = go to the position and `Hold` (2026-10-19: if there is a map trench within 6 tiles of the position, to that trench with probability `Hash01(company, faction) < trench` - automatic occupation, `DigIn` deleted), counterattacks near weak enemies. Retreat = strength < retreatStrength or average suppression > retreatSuppression -> quick step 18 tiles back for 15 s, then no re-retreat for 20 s (a strength retreat happens once - the next threshold is half).
- **Branches**: fire = if an enemy is within range+16, a **move command** (without firing) to a cover tile at range ×0.9 distance, on arrival hold = automatic mounting then fire (anti-tank uses armored enemies first as the criterion). Indirect guns = observation-only weapons use only enemies seen by officers · scouts (`SeenBySpotter`, the one closest to the front picked every 2 s by the commander) as the criterion, otherwise visible enemies; if outside range ×0.92 · inside minimum range, move to a point at range ×0.75 (not going more than 4 tiles ahead of the front) (arrival direction = toward the target) then hold -> deploy (target selection · observation limits are the engine's). If a majority of deployed guns cannot fire at the target for over 15 s (even by turning the carriage - angle to move ≥ 90°), realign toward the target in place (once per 15 s at most). Arc judgement is one place `TacticalAi.InGunArc(w, u, p)` = `World.CanTraverseInPlace` (N10). Cavalry = flank wait (front end + 14 tiles), among 8-tile blocks within 50 tiles, where infantry average suppression ≥ 0.6−0.35×cavalry or 60% or more are moving (disordered), excluding within 10 tiles of a live machine gun -> move to a flank waypoint (10 tiles beside the target) -> point charge, regroup after 12 s. Armor = attack-move to the battalion position. Wagon = follow behind the front, withdraw if an enemy is within 16 tiles. Scout = observe 10 tiles ahead of the front, withdraw if an enemy is within 12 tiles.
- **Re-command suppression** (`Retarget`): a new move command of the same action only if the goal changes by more than max(minimum, 30% of remaining distance) and 6 s have passed - the flag rebuilds the formation on every command, so petty re-commands stall the unit (the cause of the old BattleAi's 4-second re-command forever withdrawing howitzers with 6-second deployment).
- **Profiles** `assets/data/ai_profiles.json` (`AiProfiles`, read on first use, built-in defaults on failure): overrides on top of default. default · aggressive · defensive · korean · japanese · fengtian · russian-white · russian-red, aliases russian -> russian-white · soviet -> russian-red. Selection order: mission directive > `tactical:<name>` > map `factions[].aiProfile` > faction `en` > default. A copy of the profile is saved in the AI state.
- **Mission interface** `IAiDirectable.SetDirective(faction, AiPosture Attack|Defend|HoldArea, profile?, area?, radius)` - not a command (same mission = same call). Applied at organization if before the first Tick, immediately if after.
- **Save**: all AI state is `TacFaction` (integers · reals · lists) -> ObjCodec saves it with the controller (no FormatVersion change). Working buffers (`List<Unit>`, `TacGrid` = ThreadStatic) not saved. 3 snapshot scenarios added (corners4 tactical+standard, mixed engagement, just after organization).
- **Observation tools**: `--balance sim "A" "B" [map] [seed] [fog] [sec]` reads `KD_SIM_AI` (both sides, default tactical) · `KD_SIM_AI_B` (B only) and prints at the end a table of fire · melee · bombardment · deployment% · charge% by kind · tactical AI cumulative counts (`KD_TAC_DBG=1` = company state every 5 s). `--balance std [map|WxH] [AI,..] [min] [seed]` = standard game observation. `--stress ai ai=tactical` = AI thinking cost.
- **Measurement** (`--stress ai`, 16 factions × 1000 units 296²): standard AI 0.16 ms/tick -> tactical AI 0.86 ms/tick (0.81 ms per call, 4% of the whole Step 22.7 ms). 2 × 2000: 0.03 ms/tick. Mostly a CanSee sweep over all units each call.
- **Known limits (stage 1)**: on terrain maps (battle_terrain) still weak against the old AI (infantry 200 vs 200, 1 win in 4 games - flat ground 6 wins in 8). No replenishment · reinforcement unit incorporation (new units after the first organization are ignored). A battalion is an AI-internal bundle (not linked to world battalions · numbers). Trench lines · shell craters are reflected only through `TerrainCover`.

### Tactical AI stage 2 (전술 AI 2단계) (2026-10-18, ROADMAP ⑤, `src/AI/Tactical/` - `TacticalAi.Strategy.cs` · `TacticalAi.Regiment.cs` · `TacticalAi.Company.cs` · `TacMap.cs`, design `docs/TACTICAL_AI_DESIGN.md`, test `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=tactical` 60 items)
- **Layers**: faction strategy (≈2 s, per directive group `TacDirective`) -> regiment (≈1 s, `TacRegiment` = 2~4 battalions, `regimentSize`) -> battalion (≈1 s, linked to world battalion numbers) -> company (≈0.5 s). All state is in snapshot · checksum (TacticalState).
- **Rules summary**: defense · hold directive groups have no faction-level counterattack (the regiment reserve battalion makes local counterattacks - breakthrough · front battalion retreat · attack stalled). Counterattack (when no directive) = enemies within range ×1.25 in front of the position at `counterattackOdds` times or more, twice in a row -> kept for 20 s. Lift distance = `liftDistance` × range/16 (within 2× if charging). Defending company retreat = `depth` × range × 0.5 (≤ 27 tiles). An attacking company must be firing at half for over 9 s at the end of range for a fire line (before that it approaches the engagement-distance firing position), and approaches if it has fired a long time against enemies in trenches · cover. Indirect guns fire only at enemies seen by observers (scouts · officers).
- **Auxiliary AI** (`mission-aux`): a wagon does not get more than 6 tiles ahead of the escort center, and if an armed enemy within 20 tiles is seen, goes behind allies on the side opposite the threat.
- **Observation**: at the end of `--balance sim` `AI 비용: 사고 ms/틱 avg · max` (AI cost: thinking ms/tick avg · max) (controller wrapped in `StressTest.TimedAi`), `KD_TAC_DBG=1` (+ `KD_TAC_DBG_SEC=N` for `--mission-run`) = regiment phase · battalion role · company state.
- **Measurement**: `--stress ai players=4 n=1000 ticks=450` (4008 units) thinking avg 0.038 · p95 0.18 · max 1.21 ms/tick (stage 1 0.029 · 0.16 · 1.17), whole Step 3.5 ms. Engagement sim 400 units avg 0.01~0.015 ms/tick. Result table = `docs/TACTICAL_AI_DESIGN.md` §9.

### Tactical AI stage 3A - maneuver plan · mission objective · withdrawal (전술 AI 3단계 A — 기동 계획 · 미션 목표 · 철수) (2026-10-19, `src/AI/Tactical/TacticalAi.Manoeuvre.cs`, test `Tests/S3aSelfTest.cs` = `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=s3a`, results `docs/TACTICAL_AI_STAGE3_REVIEW_2026-10-19.md` '3A 결과')
- **Profile keys** (`strategy`, all on by default · bias 0 = neutral - faction character deferred): `manoeuvre` · `prePlan` (default false) · `planFrontal/Flank/Pincer/Schwerpunkt` (bias) · `maxDetour 2` · `maxArrive 120` (user decision 4) · `flankShare 0.34` · `pincerShare 0.5` · `flankMinOdds 1.2` · `pincerMinOdds 1.8` · `mgWeight` · `fixLead 20` · `objectives` · `objectiveWeight` · `withdrawAt 0` · `commitment 0.5`. **Overlay profile** `base+overlay` (`AiProfiles.Find`: the overlay overwrites only the keys written in the file) - `stage2` = stage 3A off -> stage 2 comparison with the same executable (`--ai tactical:korean+stage2`, mission `aiProfile`, `tools/bn_campaign/run.py --prof1 korean+stage2`). Difficulty goes the same way.
- **S1 plan** (regiment, `ManoeuvreStep` <- `RegimentThink` attack): after contact (enemy within 2 ranges), `ChoosePlan` once within range ×1.3 for 3 s or once per 20 s - from the enemy picture (visible enemies 45K tiles: center · front both ends · average cover · high ground · machine guns · strength ratio (visible count, capped at 3×)) scores frontal · flank (either side) · pincer (both sides, both echelons with 2 or more companies) · Schwerpunkt (the weaker zone of three enemy front zones, 3 or more enemies), strict comparison + fixed order (no randomness). Flank route = waypoint outside the enemy front end at range ×1.5 · our side ×0.9 -> assault position beside the end at range ×1.0 · our side ×0.3, passability check every 2 tiles (blocked more than 3 = none), length = per-company maximum (slot order = position along the axis), **no candidate if it exceeds length ≤ front distance × maxDetour · expected arrival (slowest member's speed × 0.45 tiles/s, forest ×1.5) ≤ maxArrive**. If contact is lost and it returns to approach, chosen again.
- **Echelons**: outer companies (by share) = maneuver (`Element=Manoeuvre`, task attack, removed from battalion placement · reserve): `ManStage` 1 waypoint -> 2 assault position (move command - no engaging on the way, if an enemy is within half range it drops out into normal engagement) -> 3 hold. The rest = fix (`Element=Fix`, role fire-based = fires only, no frame behavior, target = the enemy nearest to its assigned zone (split between the front center -> the flank end); `ManStage` 4 = waits outside enemy range ×1.15 until the maneuver's expected arrival is within `fixLead` seconds). Schwerpunkt = regiment reference point · width to that zone.
- **Synchronization** (`SyncPlan`): fix engaged (`FixTick` = at least half of the fix companies with 1/5 or more of members firing) + all maneuver arrived (`ArriveTick`) -> release (`ReleaseTick`, maneuver companies attack normally toward the enemy flank end). If the fix cannot engage 20 s after arrival, release; cancel (`PlanAborts`) when the deadline (plan + maxArrive × 1.25 + 10 s) passes or the maneuver echelon is gone. Record `TacFaction.PlanLog` (13 ints/line) -> BnCamp JSON `plans`, aggregation `tools/bn_campaign/agg_s3a.py`.
- **S14 mission objective -> task** (`ObjectiveFor`, a pure function of mission state, only in-progress · public objectives, primary objective first): capture ours = capture / enemy = hold · defend (area) ours = hold / enemy = capture (no area: enemy = destroy) · escort ours = escort (range ×0.6 ahead of the convoy) / enemy = intercept (visible tagged units) · eliminate = destroy (no spatial task). Only for the faction's default group: attack = toward the objective point when no enemy is seen, defend · hold = guard the objective area, adds to the plan score for the axis near the objective.
- **Withdrawal** (user decision 5): threshold = mission `aiWithdrawAt` > profile `withdrawAt` × multiplier, offensive limit = `culminate` × multiplier (multiplier = 2 × (1 − hold degree)). Strength ratio in contact < threshold -> `TacDirective.Withdrawn` (retreats for 30 s to range ×1.5 behind home -> defense, no counterattack, does not attack again).
- **Save**: AI blackboard (TacRegiment plan · TacGroup echelon · TacDirective withdrawal · TacFaction mission values · record, new profile keys) - ObjCodec, FormatVersion 31.

### River crossing · bottleneck breakthrough · counterattack doctrine (도하 · 병목 돌파 · 역습 교리) (crossing, 2026-10-19 user decision, `src/AI/Tactical/TacticalAi.Crossing.cs` · `TacticalAi.Counter.cs`, data `tactics.json` `crossing` · `counter`, test `Tests/CrossingSelfTest.cs` = `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=crossing`)
- **Cause of the deadlock (single bridge)**: the attacking companies' firing positions (TacMap, range × engageFrac from the enemy) fall on this bank of the river, so all fight a range-edge firefight from the bank, and the bound · charge conditions do not hold against enemies across the river - nobody was ordered to go to the bridge. (Not a frontage · formation wait · pathfinding problem.)
- **Finding the bottleneck** `FindCrossing` (a pure terrain function): the first impassable band the straight line front company -> enemy (or regiment task point) meets; axis = whichever of the two base axes crosses the band shorter, thickness `minObstacle`~`maxObstacle`, lateral length ≥ `minBarrier` (small ponds · houses are bypassed), the nearer open gap outside the band's two ends = the crossing (a bottleneck if width ≤ `maxLaneWidth` 10). If another crossing is within `secondLaneRange`, a second crossing - if `twoLanes` is false (default) it is not planned (RIV_br2 = old behavior). The yalu_crossing bridge (width 12) is not a bottleneck (widening it to 13 worsens 4/4 -> 1/4).
- **Regiment phase** (`TacRegiment.CrossPhase`, `CrossingThink` after LayOut of the RegimentThink attack task): 1 fire base (all rifle companies on riverbank firing tiles - starting from tiles with many visible enemies within react range, picked again once 6 s after the plan; the outer `fireBaseFrac` of companies = fire base (cross last); machine gun detachments = left/right of the entrance; artillery = enemies around the exit) -> after `fireMinSec`, if fire-fight progress (progress when visible enemies around the exit fall by more than `progressDrop`) is absent for `stallSec`, or those enemies × `launchOdds` ≤ crossing strength (after preparatory fire if there is artillery, `fireMaxSec` cap), 2 crossing (from the nearest tile's company at `launchMinSec` intervals nearly simultaneously at quick step, departing companies postpone their own retreat for `commitSec`) -> 3 bridgehead (crossing strength `bridgeheadFrac` crosses the exit line) -> 4 continue (crossed companies = normal attack, firing position `farSideMargin` in front of the exit line; the fire base also crosses in turn) -> 5 end. Bridgehead tile = `bridgeheadDepth` in front of the exit, first company frontal · the next toward the enemy nearer the exit (forces onto one enemy group). During the crossing · bridgehead phases, regiment · battalion collapse retreat is postponed (`CrossCommitted`). If the regiment task is not attack, the plan is erased.
- **Company stage** (`TacGroup.CrossStage`, `LineCrossing` at the start of LineThink): 1 assemble · 4 fire base = normal move to the riverbank tile -> hold (no frame), 2 crossing = quick step to the bridgehead tile (does not stop to fire - pathfinding runs the bottleneck as a column), when nearly all cross the exit line 3 bridgehead (no hold command - measured that the tail on the bridge hardens and the rear companies were blocked), 5 crossed = normal attack. Measurement: one company at a time (0.3 · 12 s) 0 wins -> nearly simultaneous 3~5 wins (fire at the bottleneck was concentrated on one company).
- **Counterattack doctrine** (`CounterThink`, mission posture `counter` = `AiPosture.Counter` (attack + doctrine) · during the faction counterattack `CounterUntil` of a defending group): contact -> reserve committed (`commitReserves`) · 1 fixed fire `fixMinSec`~`fixMaxSec` (earlier if the enemy average suppression around the target ≥ `assaultSupp`) -> if regiment front strength ≥ visible enemies × `minOdds`, 2 simultaneous assault: all companies except the fire base (`fireBaseFrac`) `CounterAssaultTick` - quick step to charge distance × `closeFrac` -> bayonet charge (`TryAssault(counter)` = conditions `chargeSuppression` · `chargeOdds`, charge-permission fallback timer `assaultFallbackSec` 15 s), postpones own retreat for `noRetreatSec` -> back to 1 at `assaultMaxSec` or if all assaulting companies retreat. No global weakening on the defending side.
- **State** (snapshot ObjCodec, FormatVersion 33): TacGroup `CrossStage` · `CrossRegiment` · `CrossLane` · `CrossPoint` · `CrossSlot` · `CounterAssaultTick`, TacRegiment `Cross*` (phase · ticks · entrance/exit · axis · width · queue · bridgehead tile · progress) · `CounterStage` · `CounterStageTick`, TacFaction cumulative `CrossPlans` · `Crossings` · `CrossLaunches` · `CrossFireBases` · `Bridgeheads` · `CounterAssaults`. As AI state, not subject to checksum · Audit (same as existing tactical AI fields).
- **Relation to the s3a maneuver plan**: while the regiment is in the crossing phase (CrossPhase ≠ 0 · ≠ end), `ManoeuvreStep` is skipped and an in-progress plan is cancelled (`EndPlan` 3) - the problem where the Schwerpunkt plan changed r.Point · width and the echelon grabbed crossing companies so no bridgehead formed (found by selftest at merge).
- **After the human merge (2026-10-19)**: crossing the bridge became slow (53/168 crossed in 40 s), so `bridgeheadMaxSec` 40 -> 70. Rechecked 3 seeds = bridge 1 rifles 3:1 2/3 · fire support 3/3 (win at 217 s) · 1.5:1 0/3 (deadlock, threshold 2.5) · counterattack 1.5:1 2/3 (recaptured at 157 s).
- **Measurement tools**: `tools/bn_campaign/gen_scen3.py` (RIV_br1/br2 × 1.5/2, RIV_br1_r2_fs fire support, RIV_br1_r3, CTR_r1/1.5/2 posture counter - old posture if `BN_CTR_POSTURE=attack`), `agg_cross.py --raw <before> --raw <after>`. Debug `KD_TAC_DBG=1` (`KD_TAC_DBG_SEC`) makes `--tactics-obs` print layer state · crossing company soldier positions.
- Multiple vehicle turrets (2026-10-19 austin, render only): hull layout `turret.mounts` = {direction: [dx,dy]} per mount point (first = `mount`), draws the same turret sheet at each mount point (from the top of the screen), common aim angle, muzzle flash alternates one turret per shot (`LodDraw.Tank fireMount`). Armored car = Austin (2 turrets, Russian green).
