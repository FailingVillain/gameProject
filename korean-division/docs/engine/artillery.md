# Engine notes - artillery (artillery: shell flight · field of fire · AI · ground fire · arty3 · FieldOps) [split from ENGINE_NOTES.md 2026-10-23, body verbatim] (엔진 노트 - artillery (포병: 포탄 비행·사계·AI·지점 포격·arty3·FieldOps))

### Shell flight time (포탄 비행 시간) (2026-10-19, `src/Core/ShellFlight.cs`, test `Tests/ShellFlightSelfTest.cs` = `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=shells`)
- **Launch** (`FireArtillery` indirect · `FireGun` direct area guns → `LaunchShell`): the impact point is decided on the launch tick (indirect spread · direct-fire miss Roll as before), muzzle recorded in `Shots`. Flight ticks = `World.FlightTicks(w, distance)` = round(clamp(flightBase + distance/shellSpeed, flightMin, flightMax) / SimDt), shellSpeed 0 = instant. At 0 ticks it goes straight to `Explode`, otherwise it is added to `World.Shells` (`ShellFlight`: shooter Id · faction · designated target Id · weapon · From · impact point · launch/impact tick).
- **Values** (balance.json, N11 2026-10-19): howitzer `shellSpeed 22 · flightBase 1.5 · flightMin 1.5 · flightMax 8` (6 tiles 1.8 s · 30 tiles 2.9 s · 60 tiles 4.2 s · 120 tiles 7.0 s; old 26/1/4 = 70 tiles 3.7 s cap 4), infantry gun `shellSpeed 60 · flightMax 1` (40 tiles 0.67 s), field gun `shellSpeed 80 · flightMax 1.2` (80 tiles 1.0 s) - always delayed with no threshold (2 tiles is 2 ticks). The whistle (`SfxDirector.ShellWhistles`) starts when remaining real time <= sound length, so even a long flight ends exactly at the moment of impact (record cleanup 20 s > flight cap 8 s). Tank guns · 37mm · anti-tank rifles are instant single-target (hit resolution is on the target entity, so as is).
- **Impact** (`UpdateShells`, every tick after `UpdateBlasts` = after unit movement, in launch order): `Explode(shooterId, owner, shooter?, from, impact point, targetId, w)` - only enemies within the radius of that "point" take damage · suppression (`Suppress`, LastFiredAtTick), crater `TryCrater(shooterId)`, `Impacts` (including weapon key) recorded. If the shooter died the damage source is null (cannot counterattack · be credited with kills), but it still explodes. The rule that a designated target hits even if neutral is kept via targetId.
- **Save · checksum**: end of CMB2 (`WriteCombat3` → `WriteShells`, weapon as key string), `HashCombat3` → `HashShells`, Audit `Shells`. FormatVersion 14.
- **Presentation**: `Impacts.Scan` - among new `Shots`, area · indirect weapons = muzzle flash + firing sound (`SfxDirector.GunFire`), `SfxDirector.ShellWhistles` = for each flying indirect shell, starts `shell_incoming` on the frame where the remaining real time to impact (`(ImpactTick − Tick) × SimDt / SimSpeed`, Game supplies speed · pause) <= whistle length (immediately if already shorter, clipped), new `Impacts` = explosion + impact sound (`ShellImpact`). The `Sfx.PlayAtLater` approximation was deleted. `ShellsInFlightNear` = query for preventing AI duplicate fire (not yet used - gun cooldown 6~11 s > flight <=3 s).

### Emplacement field of fire (방열 사계) (2026-10-19, `src/Core/Combat2.cs` '방열 사계', test `ShellFlightSelfTest.TraverseArc`)
- Weapon `traverseArc` (degrees, full width; balance.json infantry gun · field gun 45 · 3 howitzers 60, 0 = no limit) + deployable units only (`CanDeploy`). `Unit.DeployFacing` ([SnapExt], CMB2 · checksum) = center fixed when deployment started: in combat the target direction (`AdvanceDeploy(u, centre)`), for a pre-deployment with no target under a gunner order the hull direction at that time (→ the map · AI must leave it facing the enemy).
- `TraverseLocked(u)` = during deployment · deployment complete + has a field of fire. Meanwhile `FaceToward` turns only inside the field of fire via `ClampTraverse`. A gun in travel posture starts deploying after it has fully turned toward the target (|error| <= half).
- **API** `World.CanTraverseTo(unit, point)` (static): true if not bound, otherwise angle to the center <= half. `World.TraverseShiftTo(unit, point)` = angle (radians) the center must shift to bring it inside the field (0 if inside), `World.CanTraverseInPlace(unit, point)` = that angle < `combat.traverseRedeployDeg` (90°).
- **N10 re-aim (2026-10-19)**: even for a target · point outside the field of fire, if the angle to shift < 90° the **gun rotates while deployed** (`ShiftTraverse`: center `DeployFacing` and hull by the same angle, speed = turnRate × `combat.traverseShiftMul` 0.5 → infantry gun 22.5 · field gun 15 · howitzer 10 °/s; no firing meanwhile, if the angle to shift exceeds half the field then aiming restarts from the beginning, small corrections keep the aim; no state added - only `DeployFacing` changes). Common to player · AI attack orders, auto-targeting, gunner, ground fire. At 90° or more, only `Order == Attack` · ground fire go `AdvancePack` → rotate after Packed → redeploy (packTime + rotation + setupTime), otherwise (auto · gunner · attack-move) the target is dropped. Auto-targeting (`TryAcquire` → `FindTarget(..., arcFrom)`) prefers inside the field, candidates reachable by turning the gun score +5000 + angle, those at or past the threshold are excluded. Tactical AI `InGunArc` = `CanTraverseInPlace` (realignment · forced target). Measurement counters `Stats.TraverseShiftTicks` · `TraverseRepacks`. Tests `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=shells` (gunner howitzer 60° rotate in place · 150° ignored · 150° attack order redeploy) · `groundfire` (60° in place · 150° redeploy).
- Render: a selected bound gun = a faint fan out to the end of its range (`UnitRenderer`, `DeployCenter` · `TraverseHalf`).


### N11 Artillery range · spread by distance · 5 map size tiers (N11 포병 사거리 · 거리별 퍼짐 · 지도 크기 5단계) (2026-10-19)
- **Range**: infantry gun 40 · field gun 80 · howitzer 120 (old 32 · 50 · 70, minimum range unchanged - howitzer 6). Because direct guns fire farther than sight (32 · 37), they fire at **targets the faction sees (friendly observation)** when the line of fire (`los`) is clear - search radius = max(acquireRadiusCap 32, range) of `TryAcquire` (same rule as at old 50). Howitzer search radius = range (observation-only `SeenBySpotter` - scout sight x1.5 · officer).
- **Spread by distance** `World.ScatterAt(w, d)` = scatter × (near + (1 − near) × ((1 − q)·f + q·f²)), f = min(1, d/range), `combat.scatterNearFrac` 0.15 · `scatterQuad` 0.5 (multiplication · addition only - deterministic). Shared by all howitzer shells (`FireArtillery`), missed direct-gun shells (`FireGun`: splash × 0.8 + ScatterAt × √u; weapons without scatter use the old splash × 0.8~2.0), and ground fire (`FireAtGround`, × groundScatterMul 1.5). Weapon scatter: infantry gun 2 · field gun 4 · howitzer 9 (old 2.5@70 tiles). Howitzer 10 tiles 1.7 · 30 tiles 2.6 · 60 tiles 4.2 · 120 tiles 9 tiles (radius 2.5 - mostly misses at long range, for area suppression).
- **Howitzer rate of fire** 14~17 s (old 9~11). The direct-gun hit curve (`accNear/accFar`, distance/range ratio) is unchanged - at the same distance, hits rise as much as the range grew (to be observed).
- **5 map size tiers** (`Config.MapTiers` 200 · 400 · 800 · 1200 · 1500, `MaxMapSize` 1500): start screen 'Map size' (지도 크기) row (auto · tiny · small · medium · large · huge, generated maps only), `--map tiny|small|medium|large|huge` (Korean names also work). Auto = the smallest tier that fits players × `TilesPerPlayerTier` 20000 → 2 players 200 · 3~8 players 400 · 9~16 players 800 (large · huge are chosen directly). The old `Config.MapSizeFor` (2 players 150 · 10 players 336 · 16 players 424) stays for tests · command-line default · golden. Fixed maps · missions do not change size (only the load-check upper bound is 1500). Test `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=mapsizes` - generate + 60 ticks per tier: 200² 2 players generation 0.2 s · tick 0.3 ms, 800² 8 players 2.1 s · 2.8 ms, 1200² 2 players 5.8 s · 5.0 ms, 1500² 16 players 9.3 s · 10 ms (details in the N13 overload test).

### N21 Artillery AI (N21 포병 AI) (2026-10-19, branch `artyai`, `src/AI/Tactical/TacticalAi.Artillery.cs`, test `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=artyai`)
- The instruction sheet named it N20, but HANDOFF N20 was written first as gapfill, so it is recorded as **N21**. AI logic only - balance.json unchanged, no new save state (existing TacGroup fields · unit `LastShotTick` · world read-only). Thresholds in tactics.json `artilleryAi` (`ArtilleryAiDef`, `TacticRules.ArtilleryAi`).
- **Observers** (`ObserverThink`, within TacKind.Scout groups, members that are not the owning company's scout party (`ScoutCompany`) = observation teams · scout detachments - previously all `Def.Scout` were removed so observation teams stood at the start point): direct move orders one at a time (autonomous scouting off). Point = frontmost of the attack · defense rifle company center of mass in the same directive group + `observerAhead` (lateral spread evenly over the company width, redivided by the remaining count when one dies), if there are visible enemies around the point (contact) frontmost − `observerContactBack`, pushed outside rifle range × `observerStandoff` from the center of mass of visible enemies and `BestCover` (cover · high ground). If an enemy comes within rifle range it is sent back immediately. Judgment interval `observerSec`, re-ordered only when the point changes by more than `observerRepost`.
- **Observer reassignment** (`RetaskObservers`, only batteries that have observation-only guns): if the AI has no observer at all to use · knows the enemy · there has been no observation target for over 10 seconds, takes `observerRetask` men from the rifle company scout party (reserve task → rearmost company) via `FormCompany` (only scouts = ordinary company) → waiting group → company Id on the next call.
- **Howitzer target** (`PickArtilleryTarget` → `ForceFireAt`): keep a forced target if it is valid (range · observation · field of fire) and outside close danger × 0.8 (releasing it alternately costs reaction delay · re-aiming and cuts kills by 1/3 - measured). Otherwise (1) enemies within `supportRadius` of friendly assault · maneuver group · bound targets (`SupportPoints`: frame Drill.Man 4 > 1·2 > BoundSquad Drill.Enemy, for a company without a frame the nearest enemy of Assault/Charge/Bound states) (targets nearest to the battery first), (2) if a majority of auto targets are in close danger then lift fire - if the regiment has lifted (`Lifted`), around the regiment fire-power target, otherwise the nearest safe enemy, (3) otherwise the engine auto target (nearest observed enemy = preparatory fire). Close danger = a friendly unit within `dangerClose + dangerScatterMul × ScatterAt(distance)` tiles (the engine has no friendly fire so it is a rule only).
- **Preparatory fire** (`ArtilleryReady` → `PrepFired`): the regiment preparatory-fire phase ends only after the supporting battery has actually fired in that phase (with no observation, the existing cap prepSeconds + 20 s).
- **Redeployment** (shared cooldown for howitzers · direct guns `RedeployCooldown` = `redeployCooldownSec` × max(1, number of batteries / `redeployBatteryRef`), battery = howitzer group + direct-gun detachment): after deployment (Engage) during the cooldown · if any gun fired within `gunIdleSec` (for direct guns only at a target within effective range) it is not moved, a deployed battery does not correct by less than `redeployMinMove` (turn in place N10). Howitzer advance = target outside range × `redeployRangeFrac` (if firing, outside 0.92), target distance range × `redeployWantFrac`. An enemy too close (minimum range) is handled without cooldown. Realignment (outside the field of fire) is also at least the cooldown.
- **Direct guns** (`GunThink`, used instead of `FireThink` when most of a fire-power group is category gun): guns are in detachments per kind (`AddFireDetachments` - previously sniper · infantry gun · field gun were mixed in side order so the group range = sniper rifle 32, the cause of the Fengtian direct gun's first shot at 51~55 s). If a visible enemy is within range + rifle range × `gunDeployLead`, deploy; if the nearest enemy is outside effective range (× `gunEffectiveFrac`), go to a firing position (max(range × `gunStandoffFrac`, rifle range × `gunStandoffRifleMul`), behind the friendly infantry front); if blocked by the order interval (Due), wait instead of setting up as gunner (old FireThink: gunner right after attack-move → deploy → soon withdraw). **Range discipline** (`GunRangeDiscipline`): if a deployed gun has just shot a target outside effective range and there is an enemy within effective range (field of fire · line of fire), it drops the target with a gunner order → the engine picks the nearby enemy again (the engine does not change a valid target). If there is no nearby enemy, it continues at the far target.
- Machine-gun · sniper fire-power groups remain the old `FireThink` (gunner drop problem too).
- Measurement tool `ArtyFx` (KD_ARTYFX=1) has an `n20` item: observer survival (`KD_ARTYFX_OBSLOG=1` = enemies around at death), impacts before contact (infantry excluding scouts), impacts before the first infantry advance (frame bound · maneuver group · assault), gun movement start · movement gun-seconds, direct-gun far impacts that had a nearby enemy (`farAvoidable`). Results `docs/legacy/ARTILLERY_EFFECT_2026-10-19.md` 'N21'.

### N22 Artillery contribution · infantry gun · scouts (N22 포병 기여 · 보병포 · 정찰병) (2026-10-19, branch `arty2`, test `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=arty2`, HANDOFF N29)
- **Infantry gun escort** (`CloseGunThink`, `TacticalAi.Artillery.cs`, direct guns with range <= tactics.json `artilleryAi.closeGunMaxRange` 50 - branches off before `GunThink`, old rules if there is no supported company): supported company = `SupportedLine` (same battalion first, otherwise the nearest attack · defense rifle group in the same directive group). Before contact = company center of mass − advance × `closeGunFollow`, at contact (a visible enemy within range + rifle range × `closeGunContactLead` of the company) = company center of mass − enemy direction × `closeGunBehind`, pulled so that the nearest enemy is within effective range − 2, `BehindInfantry` 1 tile, and `FiringSpot` if the cell has a blocked line of fire. Movement only when changed by more than `closeGunMove`, cooldown `closeGunCooldownSec` only after firing from that deployment, not moved while firing within effective range, always back if ahead of the firing line. On arrival it issues `w.Attack` at the target immediately (a gun in travel posture deploys toward the target - a gunner deployment is fixed in the movement direction). Target `PickCloseGunTarget` (`TacGrid.BestWhere`): machine guns (spray weapons) · guns · cover (trench · `TerrainCover`) · number of enemies within 2 tiles · distance, `closeGunRetargetSec` hold · `closeGunKeep` hysteresis. `ForceGunFire` = only guns with a line of fire · within range, and if deployed but outside the field of fire, only guns that have been unable to fire for a while (redeploy).
- **Blocked-line relocation** (`BlindRelocate`, common to direct guns - cause of the Fengtian infantry gun's first shot at 120 s): deployed over `gunBlindSec` · no gun fired during that time · there are visible enemies within effective range but none with an open field of fire · direct line (`GunsBlind`) → to the nearest cell within `gunLosSearch` tiles where that enemy is in the line of fire and which is behind the friendly infantry front (`FiringSpot`) (cooldown ignored, stat `TacFaction.GunLosMoves`).
- **Howitzer values**: `redeployCooldownSec` 30 → 20, `redeployRangeFrac` 0.85 → 0.75 (sensitivity test h5, ARTILLERY_EFFECT 'N22').
- **Scout cover when hit** (정찰병 피격 엄폐) (`Scouts.cs`, balance.json `scoutAi.coverOnFire · coverRadius · coverCalmSec · coverMinSec · coverRepickSec · coverThreatRadius`): trigger = `Unit.LastFiredAtTick` within this judgment interval (15 ticks) (hit · miss · shell · machine-gun spray · grenade - a field already saved · checksummed). Both company scout parties (`ScoutThink`, before other rules unless in Return · Cover) and observation teams (company members that are scouts only, `ObserverCoverThink` at the end of `StepScouts` - even without autonomous scouting). `ScoutState.Cover` (= 4, no new field · `ScoutReportTick` = tick the cover cell was chosen · `ScoutSpot` = cover cell · read check <= 4 - FormatVersion unchanged). Cover cell `ScoutCoverSpot`: shooter = the nearest enemy within radius that has this scout as its target (otherwise the nearest enemy), score of cells within `coverRadius` = outside the line of fire (`HasFiringLine`) +40 · terrain cover +15 · distance moved away from the shooter ×2 (×4 if it gets closer) · movement −0.5/tile. On arrival gunner, if targeted again a new cell every `coverRepickSec`, `coverCalmSec` quiet + `coverMinSec` stay → company scout party = With (Return if hurt · suppressed · far), observation team = With + gunner (AI `ObserverThink` skips observers in Cover).
- **Scout sight**: `scout` unit sight/sightFar 40 → 48. `infantry_scout` is 32 × `vision.scoutSightMul` 1.5 = 48 (unchanged).
- Measurement tools: `ArtyFx` `n22` (company scout party survival · observation uptime `spotUp`/`scoutUp` · cover entries `coverIn`), `KD_ARTYFX_GUNLOG=<unit key>` (every 5 s gun position · order · deployment · field-shift angle · line of fire · number of enemies in range), `KD_ARTY2_OVR="ai.<artilleryAi key>=v,w.<weapon>.<attribute>=v,u.<unit>.<attribute>=v,scout.<scoutAi key>=v"` (measurement-run memory only). `tools/artyfx_scen/run_n22.sh` · `agg_n22.py`.

### Player ground fire (플레이어 지점 포격) (2026-10-19, `src/Core/GroundFire.cs`, test `Tests/GroundFireSelfTest.cs` = `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=groundfire`)
- **Rule** (user decision): howitzers normally fire only at enemies seen by an observer (`spotterOnly` → `SeenBySpotter`), but if the player designates a point on the ground they fire at that point without an observer. The AI does not use it (no AI code calls `World.GroundFire` - the test `AiNeverUses` confirms 0 `GroundFireCommand` · `UnitOrder.GroundFire` in a 40-second tactical AI engagement).
- **Command**: `GroundFireCommand(Faction, Units, Target)` (json `groundfire`) → `ExecGroundFire` = only guns that pass `CanGroundFireAt` per unit (howitzer or deployed direct area gun, within range · `minRange`, direct guns need a `Map.HasFiringLine` line; howitzers do not need a line) get `UnitOrder.GroundFire` (added at the very end of the enum) + `Unit.GroundPoint`. Out of range it does not follow but refuses (fires from place). `ResetOrder` (any other order: stop · move · gunner · attack etc.) cancels it and sets `GroundPoint` to 0. Included in `Immovable`.
- **Behavior** (`UpdateGroundFire`, the field-of-fire logic of `Engage` for a point): outside the field (`!CanTraverseTo`) → `AdvancePack`; in travel posture `FaceToward` → if inside the field `AdvanceDeploy(u, point direction)` → when deployment ends only the first shot waits `aimTime` (`AimTarget = -1` = aiming at a point), after that each cooldown `ConsumeAmmo` + `FireAtGround` → `LaunchShell(..., targetId 0, ...)` (LaunchShell was changed to take a `targetId` instead of an Entity). When ammo runs out (`ConsumeAmmo` fails · ammo 0) the order is cleared.
- **Spread**: indirect = `scatter × min(1, distance/range) × groundScatterMul`, direct gun = `splash × (0.3 + 0.7 × distance ratio) × groundScatterMul` (balance.json `combat.groundScatterMul`, default 1.5). Direct-gun ground fire has no hit/miss resolution.
- **Save**: Snapshot FormatVersion 21 (after merge; 20 on the standalone branch) - CMB2 `WriteCombat3` adds `GroundPoint` (X, Y) per unit, checksum `HashCombat3`, `Unit.GroundPoint` is `[SnapExt]` (passes Audit). Replay format stays 8 (only a new command kind added).
- **UI**: card `groundfire` "지점 포격" (hotkey T, 3×3 card (0,2), howitzer · infantry gun · field gun), click a point → `Game.ResolvePending`. Status text "지점 포격". While the order is alive `Game.DrawGroundFireTargets` draws a red circle (splash radius) + cross on the target, and a faint line from each selected gun to the target.
## arty3 artillery · heavy weapons bundle (arty3 포병 · 중화기 묶음) (2026-10-22, branch `arty3`, `src/Core/Arty3.cs`, FormatVersion 51)
- Values (balance.json): heavy machine guns hmg · hmg_type3 range 24 → 32 (the hit curve is by distance/range ratio so hits at the same distance rise - 24 tiles 0.12 → 0.20), field gun damage 70 → 100, howitzer splash 2.5 → 3.
- Deployment randomness: only guns (category gun) get deployment time ×(1 ± `gunDeployTimeVar` 0.15) + start delay 0~`gunDeployDelayMax` 3 s. Fixed per gun (PairNoise(Id), no state) - the delay is added to DeployLeft and consumed early in Deploying. Heavy machine-gun emplacement is unchanged.
- Hold place (`GunKeepsPlace`: gun deploying/deployed · not an attack order): blocked-line auto target → drops the target instead of withdrawing. Out-of-range auto target → waits `gunOutOfRangeHoldSec` 8 s (HoldWait), within `gunRepackCooldownSec` 20 s after deployment completes it does not chase and withdraw. Inside the field = barrel only · large angle = redeploy only on an attack order · move order = withdraws immediately (as before).
- heavy spacing (`HeavyGapStep`, Packed stage of AdvanceDeploy): if within `heavyGapWant` 5 tiles of a deploying/emplaced (-ing) friendly heavy weapon (Def.Heavy), instead of turning the hull, it is shifted sideways (perpendicular to the hull direction, opposite the nearer side) at speed ×0.5; if the minimum spacing does not grow or it is blocked (narrow road) it deploys in place. Trench spots excluded. Formation slots themselves are not touched (separate from the formation-relaxation work).
- Field gun direct + indirect (`weapon.spotterIndirect`): if there is no direct target, a second search finds targets within range seen by observer units (officer · scout); if the line is blocked, indirect fire with FireArtillery (spread ScatterAt, field gun scatter 4). Targets outside sight acquired by observation are not chased even if the line is blocked. Metric `MoveStats.SpotterIndirectShots`.
- Heavy machine-gun final line (중기관총 최후 저지선) (FireMode.FinalLine, K cycles: free → hold fire → return fire only → [if there is a heavy machine gun, choose a point] → free): `SetFinalLineCommand` (point), fire line = unit → point direction out to range, only enemies within a `finalLineBand` 2-tile band are targets (FindTarget fpl filter · FireAllowed). State Unit.FinalLinePoint. The fire line is displayed when selected (Game.DrawFinalLines).
- Save: after the ORG_ fire-control bytes, a list of (unit index, FinalLinePoint, AdjustShots, AdjustAim) (only units that have values). Test `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=arty3`.
- Howitzer adjustment fire (곡사포 조정 사격) (`adjustFireShots` 5 · `adjustFireMinMul` 1/3 · `adjustFireZone` 5 tiles): observed fire (FireArtillery) only, if within 5 tiles of the previous aim point the consecutive shot count +1 → spread 9 → 7.5 → 6 → 4.5 → 3 tiles, leaving the zone restarts from the first shot. Ground fire (no observation) does not apply. State Unit.AdjustShots · AdjustAim.

## T10 Counter-battery · flares · march fatigue (T10 대포병 · 조명탄 · 행군 피로) (FieldOps.cs, 2026-10-22, off by default)
- Data = `assets/data/fieldops.json` (`FieldOpsDefs.Current`, tests `Override`). All three features default to `enabled: false` - existing behavior · golden checksum unchanged. Draft values (before user approval).
- (2) Counter-battery: when an indirect-fire gun fires (`Fire` · `FireAtGround` → `CbOnFire`), a hostile faction that has an observation unit (Def.Spotter) within `detectRange` tiles of the gun rolls `detectChance` per shot → `World.CbMarks` (faction · gun Id · expiry tick, `revealSec`). While marked, `FogOfWar.CanSee` · `SeenBySpotter` are true → howitzers can fire at that gun. The tactical AI `PickArtilleryTarget`, if `aiPriority`, ranks a detected gun next after assault support (nearest · valid · safe).
- (3) Flares: `FireFlareCommand` (replay "flare") - officers only, `perOfficer` shots per officer (`Unit.FlaresUsed`), `range` tiles, ignored in daytime. `World.Flares` circles (`radius` · `sec`). At non-daytime, a faction with a unit within `seeRange`+radius of the circle center stamps the circle (`Stamp`) at fog update, and the hit multiplier of targets inside the circle = weather × daytime (`WeatherHitMulAt`). No AI use · on-screen display · button yet.
- (4) March fatigue: when on, walking movement drains `walkDrain`/second of stamina instead of recovering (`UpdateStamina`). Effects of exhaustion follow the existing stamina rules.
- Save = end of CMB2 `WriteFieldOps` (FormatVersion 57 reserved value, Replay 22 reserved value - re-reserve at merge), checksum `HashFieldOps` (omitted in the default state). Test `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=fieldops`.
