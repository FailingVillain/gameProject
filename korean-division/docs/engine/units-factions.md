# Engine notes - units-factions (scouts · LMG · assault infantry · bandits · new branches · engineers) [split from ENGINE_NOTES.md 2026-10-23, body verbatim] (엔진 노트 - units-factions (정찰병·경기관총·돌격보병·마적·신규 병종·공병))

### Scouts (정찰병) (2026-10-19, ROADMAP '정찰병 유닛', `src/Core/Scouts.cs`, test `Tests/ScoutSelfTest.cs` = `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=scouts`)
- **Data**: unit `infantry_scout` (balance.json, `scout: true` + `spotter: true`; the old `scout` unit stays as is for StandardAi - no sight multiplier · no autonomous behavior). `Company.Scouts` (in Id order, separate from `Members` · squads · flag formation), `Unit.ScoutCompany` (back-reference), `Unit.ScoutAuto` · `ScoutState` (`With` with the company · `Advance` moving · `Observe` observing · `Return` returning) · `ScoutSpot` (last autonomous move target) · `ScoutReportTick` (tick of first seeing an enemy ahead, -1 = none) · `ScoutResumeTick`. Public API (tai2 etc.): `Unit.IsScout` · `Unit.ScoutState` · `Unit.ScoutAuto` · `Unit.ScoutCompany` · `Company.Scouts` · `World.SetScoutAuto(f, units, on)`.
- **Organization**: `ExecFormCompany` separates `Def.Scout` units in the list into a scout party (cap `scoutAi.perCompany` 2; if there are ONLY scouts it is an ordinary company; overflow = no company), excluded from `InitialStrength` · squads · formation. `LeaveCompany` (death · reorganization) also removes from the scout party, disbanding = detach + autonomy off, annihilation = detach (autonomy kept). Unattached `ScoutAuto` scouts are, every 30 ticks, joined by `AttachLooseScouts` into a vacant slot of the nearest same-faction company within `attachRadius`(40) tiles (handles combat test / quick battle / companies the AI formed later). Standard starting force is `infantry_scout: 2` per company bundle in balance `start.force`.
- **Tick stage** `StepScouts` (Step: after StepGapChecks · before unit update): per scout every 15 ticks (spread by Id). Company situation (`BuildScoutCtx`) = center of mass · anchor (living officer, otherwise center of mass) · activity (flag marching / 1/3 or more of members moving / members in combat) · advance direction (flag target direction → average target of moving members → `Facing` → target direction). Movement is the same single-man `MoveGroup` + `Order = Move` as normal movement (no command · reaction delay · replay record - a function of tick · state, so same input = same result). While observing, `Order = Hold`.
- **Observation point** `ScoutPickSpot`: reference point = company center of mass + direction × (aheadMin~aheadMax by unit Id hash + `marchLead` while marching) + lateral spacing `spread` within the scout party; in the surrounding `searchRadius` tiles (at least aheadMin+2 ahead of the company · within maxDist−1 of the company center) score = −distance² + high ground 30 + forest edge 20 + cover 5 − deep forest 40 (water · barbed wire excluded, ties go to the cell scanned first). A scout observing while standing that falls behind aheadMin (company catches up) skips to the next point → about 6 tiles ahead on average while marching.
- **Return** (during either Advance · Observe): suppression >= `returnSuppression` 400 · HP <= `returnHpFrac` 50% · `reportSec` 5 seconds after first seeing an enemy ahead (enemy = hostile unit within its own `SightOf` · `Fog.CanSee`, per scout) · more than `maxDist` 15 tiles from the company center of mass → `Return`: moves to the anchor, becomes `With` once within `arriveDist`. Restart conditions: `cooldownSec` 30 seconds elapsed · suppression < `resumeSuppression` · HP > 50% · company active. In `With`, follows when it strays `followDist` or more from the anchor.
- **Direct order = autonomy off**: Move · AttackMove · QuickStep · Attack · Stop · Hold · Charge resolve their targets via `ResolveOrder` - if a scout is mixed into the same order as company members it is treated as a company order and the scout is removed from the order, keeping autonomy; if the scout is alone (including unattached scouts) `ScoutAuto = false`; when a company member receives an order, the switched-off autonomy of that company's scout party is turned back on (the AI's company orders take the same path). Only the card toggle `SetScoutAutoCommand` (E, `자율 정찰`) explicitly turns it on and off. On the AI side: `TacticalAi` does not gather `Def.Scout` units into a detachment (one line), and `MissionAi.KeyOf` puts scouts into their company's bundle.
- **Pass-through**: scouts moving autonomously (`Separate` flag 16) do not push each other in friendly overlap resolution (a dense column cannot be pushed through by body, so they would get trapped inside the company). Standing (Hold) scouts are treated as ordinary standing units.
- **Howitzer observation**: scouts are `spotter` units → `SeenBySpotter` counts them as is with `SightOf(scout)` (x1.5) (no code change, confirmed by test).
- **Save**: end of ORG_ (`WriteScouts`: per scout Id · company Id · autonomy · state · target · report tick · restart tick), membership · faction validated on load. Checksum `HashScouts` (end of HashOrganisation) + scout party Ids per company. `Snapshot.FormatVersion` 17 (after merging to mainline). Replay format unchanged (one added command `scoutauto`).
- **x1.5 adjustment (at merge)**: all scoutAi distances x1.5 to match the map x1.5 - aheadMin/Max 9~15 · marchLead 3 · spread 7.5 · searchRadius 4 · maxDist 22 · followDist 12 · arriveDist 6 · attachRadius 60 (6~10 · 15 · 40 in the text above are old values). Scout sight 32/20/32 (infantry x4/3 rule) × 1.5, carbine = mainline carbine (range 22). FormatVersion 17.
- **Not implemented**: prone-scout detection x0.7 (concealment is the probabilistic `UpdateDetection` so there is no clean hook to put a distance multiplier in - if needed, design separately by putting a posture multiplier in `SightOf` and `SeenBySpotter`), mounted scouts, scout-only selection UI (selecting a company does not select its scout party - scouts are clicked individually).

### LMG fire direction · sprite alignment (경기관총 사격 방향 · 스프라이트 정합) (N2 / HANDOFF 4o, 2026-10-19, test `Tests/LmgAimSelfTest.cs` = `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=lmgaim`)
- **Cause (investigation)**: the sim had no problem - an emplaced LMG fires only after turning to the target bearing because of `AimedAt` (deployed units ±5° of body direction), and rotation is 14 rad/s by default (practically instant). The misalignment was entirely on the art side: (1) the fixed lateral swing in game-tools `bases/infantry_heavy.py` `MG_SWING = {e: -80, se: -42, ne: -98}` (a trick to fit the gun inside the 128 cell) made the mg_aim barrel deviate from the row direction by se 34° · e 69° · ne 82° (mg_down e 58°), (2) the 36° camera elevation projection makes a horizontal barrel appear at 30° on screen in diagonal rows (the map is a plane seen from above so screen bearing must equal world bearing), (3) 8-direction quantization (max 22.5°) + old direction dead-band 12° → difference between drawn and actual direction up to 34.5°.
- **Art (game-tools `lmg-art`)**: the gun pose is left as the s pose in all directions (0 penetration), and the whole figure is rotated about the ground contact point by `view_yaw` (`voxel.render_figure`) so that **the projected barrel axis matches the row direction exactly** (`_swing_to_screen`, mg_kneel · mg_down · mg_aim solutions, firing cells = aim solution + recoil only → <= 1.3°). Check `python image/tools/mg_axis.py <unit>`. The 5 LMG kinds use `"dirs": "16"` (9 rows s · sse · se · ese · e · ene · ne · nne · n + mirror = 16 directions, `voxel.YAWS` 22.5° keys, `common.DIRS16`) · `"frame_wide": 1.5` (cells 192×160 / 96×80 / 48×40, zoom · anchor height identical - `unit_loader.WideRes`, LOD file tags stay @64/@32).
- **Game render**: `LodSprite` uses the layout's dirs count · cell size as is so no code change (9 rows = equal 22.5° split). `UnitRenderer.ShownFacing` replaced by **row hysteresis**: shown direction = row center, moved only when the actual direction leaves the current row cell (half-width + `RowHysteresis` 4°) (`SnapFacing`, public for tests). Max error = half-width + 4° (infantry 5 rows 26.5°, LMG 9 rows 15.25°; if first drawn at the moment of firing <= 11.25°).
- **Sim rule**: `UnitDef.traverseRate` (deg/s, 0 = turnRate) = aim rotation speed while emplacing · emplaced (`World.FaceToward`). LMG 90°/s - rotates to aim while emplaced behind a sandbag parapet, no field-of-fire limit · no re-emplacing (opposite-side target = 2 seconds of rotation + aiming). The alternative (field of fire + re-emplacing) was not adopted because the parapet is baked into the art per row so there is no visual gain, and auto-targeting ignores enemies outside the field of fire so the flank would be defenseless. No new state (FormatVersion unchanged), golden updated.
- Scene for checking: `KD_SCENE=mgaim KD_AIMDEBUG=1 --demo` = LMGs surrounding one target from 16 bearings, red = target bearing, yellow = drawn row direction.
### N21 Assault infantry = carbine + 2 grenades · Korean army only (N21 돌격보병 = 카빈 + 수류탄 2 · 한국군 전용) (2026-10-19 user decision, branch `carbine`)
- **Weapon**: submachine gun `smg` removed. New `carbine_assault` (range 16, damage 35, interval 1.7 ±0.2, accuracy 0.38/0.06, suppress 45). The existing `carbine` (scouts · cavalry) is unchanged - the names overlap so `carbine_assault`. Role `tactics.json` / `CombatFormations` byWeapon: `carbine_assault` → maneuver group.
- **Korean army only**: unit definition `koreaOnly` (`UnitDef.KoreaOnly`). `MissionDef.Validate` errors on organizations of a faction with `en` != "korean" ("한국군 전용"). 19 units leaked into Fengtian were replaced with `infantry_fengtian` (mission + tools copies). Audit `MissionSelfTest.KoreaOnlyScan` (scans all missions + tools/ scenario JSON + a negative test). Outside missions (`--balance sim`, quick battle) it is not checked.
- **Assault fire default ON**: `marchFireDefault` - when forming a company, if a majority of members are that unit, `Company.MarchFireOn` starts on (snapshot already saves it). The tactical AI `Issue` always turns it on for this company (even with officers mixed in). Per-unit march multipliers `marchAccMul` 0.7 (rifle combat.marchAccMul 0.4) · `marchIntervalMul` 1.2 (rifle 1.5). 0 = global value.
- **Close-range sidearm**: unit `sidearm` (`pistol_assault`: damage 20 · range 6 · interval 0.8~1.0) + `sidearmRange` 6. `World.UpdateSidearm` (Combat4.cs) switches `Unit.Weapon` between main weapon <-> sidearm every tick by target distance (enter <= 6, leave > 6.5 hysteresis; main weapon if no target). No new state - the `Unit.Weapon` key is already in the snapshot · checksum. Bayonet charge keeps the existing `melee: sabre_short`.
- Known limitation: because of the existing behavior where on an attack order both rifles and carbines walk toward the target and stop to shoot at 6~7 tiles, they often actually fight in the pistol band (pistol hit curve accFar 0.05 → low at the end of 6 tiles). March fire depends on officer judgment + no-target conditions so its real-use ratio is low (EXCHANGE_TABLE N21).

## Bandits · common units · rifle variants (마적 · 공용 유닛 · 소총 변형) (N26 · N53 · N54, 2026-10-19, branch `factions`)
- **Generalized faction restriction**: `UnitDef.Factions` (list of map factions[].en, case-insensitive) + `Common` (allowed to all factions, highest priority) + existing `KoreaOnly`. Ruling = `UnitDef.AllowedFor(en)`, used by mission validation (`MissionDef.Validate`). Restricted · common units are left out of the default AI organization candidates (`AiDefs`) (so same-rated units do not change the order).
- **Bandits (마적) (`bandit`)**: 4 unit kinds `bandit_thug` (broadsword only · `meleeOnly`) · `bandit_rifleman` (= infantry) · `bandit_horseman` (= mounted rifleman) · `bandit_leader` (= officer). All `factions:["bandit"]`. `meleeOnly` = main weapon = melee weapon (broadsword, range 0.3) + `ChargeOnly` is turned on at spawn → no shooting (stays an armed unit so the AI engages; a null weapon is treated as "unarmed" so it is not used). The `ranks.json` bandit rank table already exists. Test mission `assets/missions/bandit_raid.json` (35 bandits vs Korean company 31, AI vs AI).
- **Art substitute**: `UnitDef.ArtAs` / `ArtVariant` - if the theme has no `unit.<key>`, `unit.<artAs>@<artVariant>` → `unit.<artAs>` (UnitArt.For). Bandits = Fengtian infantry · cavalry · officer art, team color from mission factions[].color. If dedicated art arrives as `unit.bandit_*` it takes priority automatically. The bandit variant of `FactionArt` is null.
- **Common units (`common`)**: `"common": true` → can be organized by every faction. Team color · rank table (default fallback) · art (`artAs`) follow the owning faction. Only 1 placeholder `common_volunteer_tmp` ("의용병 (임시)", regular army infantry values) - the real spec comes from the user later (Gando Korean volunteers · Mongol cavalry).
- **7 rifles + faction distribution (N54)**: details `docs/WEAPONS_RIFLES.md`. A `factionRifles` weight table picks the rifle of rifle-slot infantry (weapon `rifle_mosin`, common excluded) deterministically in `World.SpawnUnit` by unit id · map seed Weyl sequence (`Defs.WeaponKeyFor`). Can be fixed with mission force `weapon`. Weapon `ammoType` data field. Saving keeps the `Unit.Weapon` key as is so no snapshot format change (FormatVersion kept). `infantry_default_old` = Berdan II Korean old-style infantry. Limitation: the close-range sidearm (`UpdateSidearm`) reverts to `Def.Weapon` (assault infantry are not in rifle slots → no effect).
- Test: `Tests/FactionsSelfTest.cs BanditCommonRifles` (`KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=factions`).

- Standard bearer (기수) (`Core/Bearer.cs`, combat.bearer*): break probability of same-faction soldiers within a radius of 10 tiles x0.7 (`BreakChanceOf`), AI company suppression retreat roll x0.85 (a living bearer in the company). When he falls (RemoveDead), soldiers within the radius get `Unit.BearerFellUntil` (20 seconds) → break · retreat roll x1.3. When the break switch (breakChance) is off only the AI retreat side works. Saved in CMB2 · checksum.
- Cavalry scout dismounted observation (`Dismount.cs`): dismounts automatically when observing while standing (`ScoutObserving`), remounts on a move order. In sight `SightOf` the dismounted state is x`dismountSightMul`.
- Art: `artAs` provisional (Mongol · Cossack · scout = cavalry, volunteers = reservists, guard = Fengtian infantry, bearer = infantry, tachanka = Maxim), dedicated art takes priority automatically when it arrives in the theme.
- Tachanka usable by the Korean army confirmed (2026-10-22): definition `factions: korean/russian/soviet` · art `heavy-tachanka` already has Korean uniforms (kepi · gray, `docs/art_review/tachanka_korea.png`).
