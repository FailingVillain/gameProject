# Engine notes - suppression-surrender (suppression redesign and surrender) [split from ENGINE_NOTES.md 2026-10-23, body verbatim] (엔진 노트 - suppression-surrender (제압 재설계와 항복))

### Suppression redesign N25 (제압 재설계 N25) (2026-10-19 user decision, branch `supp`, `src/Core/Suppression.cs`, test `Tests/SuppressionSelfTest.cs` = `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=supp`, **FormatVersion 30**)
- Rationale · measurements: `docs/legacy/BATTALION_CAMPAIGN_2026-10-19.md` '제압 · 사기 재설계를 위한 근거' · '제압 재설계 결과'. The suppression section of 'Combat overhaul round 2' above (decay fixed at 80/s · no effect on hit chance) is replaced by this section.
- **Near-miss impact** (`NearMissPath` · `NearMissRing`, one spatial-hash lookup, independent of hash order - integer addition + cap is commutative): the line of fire of a missed direct-fire shot (`Fire` rifle · carbine · pistol · sniper · anti-tank rifle, `FireSpray` machine gun) = `nearMissPathLen` 6 tiles before the impact point + `nearMissOvershoot` 3 tiles beyond, and an enemy within (edge distance) d < `nearMissRadius` 1.5 tiles of that segment gets weapon suppress × `nearMissFrac` 0.5 × (1 − d/1.5) (resistance · cover applied). The target (direct suppression) and units inside the machine-gun spray circle (impact-point spray radius, full amount as before) are excluded. Explosions (cannon `Explode` · grenade `UpdateBlasts`) stay full inside the radius + an outer 1.5-tile band (same falloff, grenades ignore cover). Allies · neutrals excluded, `LastFiredAtTick` is not changed (keeps the definition of "hit" for ammo recovery · measurement).
- **Gain · decay** (reflecting N39 2026-10-19 user decision "제압이 너무 빨리 빠짐"): gain = suppress × resistance × cover × (1 − `suppressGainSat` 1.0 × suppression ratio) (saturating - does not stick to the cap, `AddSuppression`), decay = `suppressDecay` **25**/s (old 80, proportional term `suppressDecayProp` 0 - code kept) × (1 + rank × `suppressRankDecayBonus` 0.05) × (near an officer `officerDecayMul` 1.25 (old 3), otherwise NCO squad `ncoDecayMul` 1.25) × (if targeted within the last `suppressCalmSec` 4 seconds (`LastFiredAtTick` - hit · miss · spray · explosion, not near-miss) `suppressFireDecayMul` 0.25). Recovery: 0.6 → 0.4 takes 11.2 seconds after fire stops (8.5 seconds when starting from a calm state, test `TestDecay`). Sustained-fire equilibrium S* ≈ 1 − 6.25/g → a squad under a machine gun stays pinned (`TestMgKeepsPinned`: 1 Maxim at 16 tiles, 8 riflemen pinned 53%, max 0.95). Weapon suppress (N39 readjustment, same as the old values): rifle · long rifle 60, carbine 50, assault carbine 45, LMG 48 (old 40), heavy MG 36 (old 30) (cannon · grenade · pistol · sniper unchanged). The 1.5x values of the first commit (90/75/68/60/45) were too strong when combined with decay 25 (engagement p90 0.79 · pinned 27%) → lowered.
- **Hit chance**: `HitChance` shooter × `SuppressAccuracyMul` = (1 − `suppressAccMul` 0.5 × suppression ratio) × (if pinned `pinnedAccMul` 0.75). Fire-interval multiplier (×(1 + suppression ratio)) unchanged.
- **Pinned (prone)** (고정 (엎드림)) `Unit.Pinned` ([SnapExt], CMB2 per unit Bool after Retreating, HashCombat3 bit 2): `UpdatePinned` (UpdateUnitCore, every tick right after suppression decay) - suppression ratio >= `pinAt` 0.6 pins, < `pinRelease` 0.4 releases (hysteresis). Targets `World.CanPin` = infantry on foot (excluding cavalry · guns · vehicles · suppression-immune officers). When pinned, ends the charge state and forbids auto-charge (`AutoChargeAllowed`). `PinnedHold` (after ReactDelay · target cleanup, before the order switch): unless it is a retreat order (`IsFleeing`) · gunner · ground fire, the unit does not move and only shoots enemies inside weapon range (a designated attack target is kept even out of range, the order · group is kept → resumes when released). A flag formation naturally becomes "company pinned" through the lagging-member rule (halts when more than a third lag). Being hit: `TerrainAccuracy` cover multiplier = min(terrain, `proneHitMul` 0.6) (does not stack with trench 0.6). Art: `UnitArt` prone aim/fire (aim_prone/fire_prone) = `u.Pinned`, retreat movement while pinned = crawl. Squad icon `SquadIcons.Facts.Pinned` = half or more of the squad pinned (top priority).
- **Officer suppression retreat** (장교 제압 후퇴) (`TacticalAi.CompanyThink`, tactical AI only - player companies have no engine retreat rule so only pinning applies): casualty threshold (`retreatStrength`, 30~40%) as is + a roll every judgment (0.5 seconds). The suppression the officer sees `ContactSuppression` = average of contacted soldiers (infantry excluding the officer, attacked within 5 seconds) (if contacted soldiers < max(3, 25%) then the average of all infantry). Hazard rate/s `RetreatHazard` = `retreatSuppRate` 0.10 × sf × (1 + `retreatCasualtyWeight` 1.5 × casualty rate/(1 − retreatStrength)), sf = (s − `retreatSuppStart` 0.3)/(`retreatSuppression` − 0.3) (0..2; default profile 0.6, aggressive 0.75 · 0.7, cautious 0.5), × officer max(0.3, 1 − 0.1 × experience level − 0.05 × (rank − 1)) / NCO deputy ×1.5 / no command ×1.5. Probability = min(1, hazard × 0.5), random number `RetreatRoll` = hash (tick · faction · group · map seed). A suppression retreat does not turn on Spent (halved threshold). Records `TacGroup.RetreatCause` (1 strength · 2 suppression), `TacFaction.FallbacksSupp` (AI blackboard = saved).
- **AI threshold**: `tactics.json superioritySupp` 0.20 → 0.30 (also the TacticDrill default). Measurement 'BATTALION_CAMPAIGN 제압 재설계 결과'.
- **Measurement hooks**: `MoveStats.NearMissHits · PinEvents · PinnedTicks`, BnCamp `pinsec.eng/all` · event columns `cause` · `supC`, counter `fallbacksSupp`. Tool `tools/supp_exchange.py` (N18 key row).

### Suppression redesign 2 (제압 재설계 2) (supp, 2026-10-20, `src/Core/Suppression.cs`, test `KD_SELFTEST_ONLY=supp`, **FormatVersion 41**)
- **Break rule turned off (2026-10-20, user decision "붕괴 규칙은 굳이 넣지 않고 싶은데")**: `breakChance` default 0 (balance.json · Defs) - break · stopping fleeing at water's edge · `breakCorneredMul` are dormant (code kept, re-enabled if the value is > 0). Prone 0.35 · cover recovery ×1.25 stay on. Break tests are explicitly activated with `BreakOnScope`.
- On top of the existing N25 (near-miss · hit chance · pinned · officer retreat roll), **3 switches (balance.json `combat`, all off by default - awaiting user decision)**:
  - **Prone** (엎드림) `proneAt` (proposed 0.35) · `proneRelease` 0.2 · `proneSettleSec` 0.5: an unpinned infantryman on foot who is standing (LastMoveTick) and not charging · broken goes `Unit.Prone` at suppression ratio >= proneAt → being hit = min(terrain, proneHitMul). Pinned = always Prone. Art = prone shooting.
  - **Cover recovery** (엄폐 회복) `coverDecayMul` (proposed 1.25): suppression decay multiplier on tiles where the terrain suppression multiplier < 1.
  - **Break** (붕괴) `breakChance` (proposed 0.15/`breakInterval` 1 second) × (s − `breakAt` 0.85)/(1 − 0.85) × max(0.2, 1 − rank × 0.08) × near officer 0.4 / NCO 0.6 × cover 0.5 × (1 + 2 × company casualty rate) (`BreakChanceOf`). Soldiers who are pinned + s >= breakAt (excluding emplaced-weapon gunners · officers · cavalry) roll (hash Roll) → `BrokenUntil` · `BrokenAt` · `BreakTo` (opposite side of the nearest visible enemy, `breakDistance` 10 tiles). While broken, `BrokenFlee` (UpdateUnitCore, before PinnedHold): does not shoot, goes to BreakTo (offsets the suppression slowdown), order · group kept, retreat orders (IsFleeing) are followed. After `breakMinSec` 8 seconds recovers if s < `breakRallyAt` 0.3, unconditionally at `breakMaxSec` 40 seconds.
- Save = CMB2 per unit Prone · BrokenUntil · BrokenAt · BreakTo after Pinned, checksum HashCombat3 (bit 8 + fields). Measurement = `MoveStats` per-faction ProneTicks · PinTicksF · BrokenTicks · Breaks · Rallies · SuppBy (source SupSrc direct/spray/near-miss/explosion/grenade/cavalry, AddSuppression records the actual increase) → tactics-obs `[제압2]` line.
- Measurement (tactics-obs A/B/C seeds 1~5 · sim RR rifle 100 vs 100 · LT rifle 92 + LMG 8 attacking vs trench rifle 100, seeds 1~5, `tools/supp_run.sh` · `supp_agg.py`, raw data docs/supp_raw):
  - main: A win 3/5 K/E 0.91 · B 3/5 0.98 · C 5/5 0.57, timeouts 1/1/0, S1 Korean assault retreat after 40 seconds 12/22 = 55%, LT attack wins 1/5 (timeout 4), RR A wins 1/5.
  - all three on: A 2/5 1.04 · B 3/5 0.93 · C 5/5 0.61, timeouts 1/1/0, S1 10/27 = 37%, LT 2/5 (timeout 3), many breaks (5~20 per faction per game).
  - break only on: A 2/5 1.04 · B 3/5 1.00 · C 5/5 0.61, timeouts 1/2/0, S1 11/21 = 52%, LT **3/5** (timeout 2), breaks 3~12 per game, fleeing 60~190 man-seconds.
- (At the 1st round) why they were off by default (selftest): prone on → tactical test howitzer fired 0 rounds (did not deploy) · together with break the AI-match tank stalled 5 seconds · standard AI victory test failed; break on → crossing bridgehead 0 men (collapsed on the bridge and turned back) · mission withdrawal (the attack did not reach 85%); cover recovery on → mission withdrawal failed. Turning them on requires matching adjustments on the AI side (crossing · artillery · withdrawal).
- **Round 2 (supp2, 2026-10-20, user approval - all three on by default: proneAt 0.35 · coverDecayMul 1.25 · breakChance 0.15, Defs defaults the same)**. Cause fixes:
  - Howitzer 0 rounds (prone): as infantry engagements lengthened, howitzers that were deploying for 20 seconds inside rifle range after friendly infantry pulled back died to concentrated fire before shooting → `ArtilleryThink`: a howitzer battery with no deployed gun, if a visible enemy is within `ExposedStandoff` 30 tiles, folds the deployment (even in progress) and withdraws `ExposedRetreat` 50 tiles from that enemy to deploy (TacticalAi.Artillery.cs, 0 = off).
  - Break flight crossed bridges · rivers: `BreakEscape` - follows the flight straight line at 0.5-tile steps and cuts it before the tile that reaches a water tile (bridge · riverbank); if cut (back to the wall · bridgehead) the break probability × `breakCorneredMul` 0.5 (balance.json). Roll order: roll after computing the flight path (same hash).
  - AI-match tank 5-second stall (prone + break changed the match flow, tank amid a waiting friendly crowd): `CheckProgress` reset the stall level to 0 by the displacement of a sidestep interval, so it never reached stepping aside (stage 3) → if a large unit sidestepped in that interval and a waiting friendly is right ahead, it is judged as net advance toward the steering point; this judgment raises only up to stage 3 (give-up stage 5 uses only the old judgment - so a Maxim on a bridge does not give up and block the road). The standard AI victory test also passes.
  - Mission withdrawal · crossing bridgehead: on top of main (bal2 · stale etc.) they pass with all three on (bridgehead around 125 men).
  - Measurement: docs/BASELINE_CURRENT.md 'supp2' block (same build switches off vs on, tactics-obs A/B/C · sim RR/LT seeds 1~5, raw data docs/legacy/supp2_raw).

## N79b Surrender (항복) (2026-10-20, branch `surr`, `src/Core/Surrender.cs`)
- **User spec (quote)**: "소속된 중대가 대부분 소탕되어, 소수의 인원만 남았을때, 퇴각하지 못하고 강한 제압을 받고 있는 상황이라면, 굉장히 까다로운 조건 하에 항복한다. 항복한 유닛은 만세 포즈를 취하며, 그 자리에서 움직이지 않는다. 우선 AI는 퇴각을 먼저 시도하며, 플레이어의 유닛은 퇴각보다는 명령을 수행하는 데 집중한다. 플레이어의 유닛 역시 항복할 수 있다. 단, AI보다 더 적은 빈도로 항복한다."
- **Judgment (every tick `UpdateSurrender`, after UpdateBreak, all JSON `combat.surrender*`)**: (1) infantry on foot · suppression target (`CanPin`, excluding officers) · belongs to a company, company current strength <= starting strength × (1 − `surrenderCasualty` 0.8) and <= `surrenderMaxLeft` 10 men. (2) Suppression ratio >= `surrenderSuppAt` 0.85 for `surrenderSuppSec` 10 seconds in a row (`Unit.SuppHighTicks`, reset to 0 when it drops below). (3) **Cannot retreat (`World.CannotRetreat`)**: there is a non-surrendered enemy infantry within the threat radius `surrenderThreatRadius` 20 tiles, and (a) encirclement = magnitude of the sum of enemy→me unit vectors (weighted 1/distance) / sum of weights < 0.35, or (b) the straight lines of `surrenderProbeLen` 10 tiles in three directions - opposite the enemy · left and right `surrenderProbeDeg` 60° - are all blocked (map edge · unwalkable tile · tile adjacent to water (`NearWater`) · enemy within `surrenderBlockRadius` 4 tiles of the line). With no enemy it is not "cannot retreat". (4) Every `surrenderInterval` 2 seconds (spread by Id) a `surrenderChance` 0.05 hash roll; the player faction (`Faction.IsAi` false) × `surrenderPlayerMul` 0.3. `surrenderChance` 0 = off.
- **AI = retreat first**: if condition (3) says retreat is possible, it does not surrender and follows the existing tactical AI retreat · withdrawal logic (the engine does not retreat it separately). **Player** = no automatic retreat by this rule (carries out orders), surrenders only under the same conditions + a lower probability.
- **Surrendered state (`Unit.Surrendered`)**: Hp kept (alive), `HoldSurrendered` clears movement · order · group · target · charge every tick → does not move, does not shoot, ignores orders. Excluded from the enemy's `FindTarget` · `IsValidTarget` (both auto and designated attacks), excluded from capture · victory/defeat · mission survivor count (`aliveCount` · `m.Alive` · engagement victory/defeat) · result `Survivors` = counted as prisoner (loss), `MissionResults.FactionLine.Captured` · `MoveStats.Surrenders[faction]` · tactics-obs `[항복]` line. Not excluded: area damage (cannon · grenade explosion) can still hit them.
- **Save**: CMB2 per unit `Surrendered` · `SuppHighTicks` + checksum (HashCombat3) + `[SnapExt]` audit, Snapshot.FormatVersion 43.
- **Art**: game-tools `anims/infantry.json` · `infantry-motions.json` get `surrender` (surrender_1/2, 600 ms sway), pose `bases/infantry_motions.py surrender()` (rifle laid at the feet with `drop`, both arms raised beside the head). Game `UnitArt.aSurrender` (added to LodSprite.UsedAnimNames), units without frames use idle.
- **Proposals (not implemented this time)**: escort · prisoner handling (prisoner count to campaign result · link to foreign units N71), surrender demand (player command), surrenderers released when friendlies approach, tactical AI excluding surrenderers from threat · combat strength counts, exemption from area damage, officer · heavy-weapon gunner surrender, surrender voice · sound effect, surrender icon (squad icon state), reflecting morale · rank in surrender probability.
