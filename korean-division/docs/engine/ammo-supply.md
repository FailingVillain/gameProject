# Engine notes - ammo-supply (given forces, ammo, supply wagons) (엔진 노트 - ammo-supply (주어진 병력·탄약·보급 마차)) [split from ENGINE_NOTES.md 2026-10-23, body verbatim]

### Direction change §0-1: given forces, ammo regeneration, supply wagon, cover (방향 변경 §0-1: 주어진 병력 · 탄약 회복 · 보급 마차 · 엄폐) (2026-10-19, `src/Core/Supply.cs` · `SupplyUnit.cs`, tests `Tests/SupplySelfTest.cs` · `Tests/CoreV2SelfTest.cs`)
- **Deleted**: 5 resource types · `Fixed` resource table · faction stock/income/population · production · construction · repair · engineers (worker) · supply depots · fuel tanks · fuel · automatic resupply trips · engineer refueling · turrets · factories/labs · resource transfer · funds cheat. `Building` = cities and captured sites only (balance `buildings`: metropolis · city · village · mine · oilfield, all capturable · indestructible). Commands `Build/Repair/Train/CancelTrain/SetRally/ToggleProduction/AssignBuilder/Refuel/TransferResource/DebugFunds` deleted -> `ReplayFile.FormatVersion` 3, `Snapshot.FormatVersion` 7.
- **Given forces**: map json `"units": [{ "unit", "at": [x,y], "faction", "count" }]` -> `GameMap.UnitSpawns` (stored in the snapshot). If absent, `balance.start.force` (unit count per bundle; `company: true` means company organization · `battalion`) is placed in front of the metropolis (`World.SpawnStartForce`).
- **Defeat**: 0 cities (city flag) or all units wiped out -> eliminated (`WorldCapture.UpdateVictory`). With no metropolis for `start.revealDelay` seconds -> position revealed. The engagement test keeps the wipe-out rule as is.
- **Ammo regeneration** (`World.UpdateAmmo`): one shot = 1 round (`ConsumeAmmo`). After `supply.regenDelay` (10 s) since the last shot · being hit · being attacked (including misses, `Unit.LastFiredAtTick` = on entering Combat3.Suppress), every `supply.interval` (1 s), only for units with `(Tick + Id) % interval == 0`: cap × `regenPct` (1%) × interval × multiplier; amounts under 1 round accumulate in `AmmoFrac`. Multiplier = within `cityRadius` (8) of the edge of an own/allied city ×`cityMul` (3), or within `wagonRadius` (6) of an own supply wagon with cargo left ×`wagonMul` (3) - the larger one only. The engagement test (default) has unlimited ammo.
- **Supply wagon** (`supply_wagon`, `cargo` 40 loads, 1 load = a full ammo cap for 1 soldier): unarmed · cannot capture. When the wagon accelerates regeneration, `Unit.Cargo` decreases by the accelerated amount (`regenPct·interval·(mul−1)`); within `cityRadius` of an allied city it reloads at `wagonRefill` loads/s. The art is a vehicle alias (no dedicated sheet).
- **Cover**: tile terrain only (`GameMap.TerrainCover` - the best value among forest · rubble · trench terrain · shell crater · beside a house). Hit = `Combat2.TerrainAccuracy` -> `CoverHitMul`, suppression = `Combat3.Suppress` × `CoverSuppressMul`. **Construction (personal trench `DigInCommand` · trench line `DigLineCommand` · digging state · `cover` section) was deleted 2026-10-19** (user decision - trenches are only terrain drawn on the map/mission; automatic occupation is in "참호 자동 점유" (trench auto-occupation) below).
- **AI**: (old AI - kept only for comparison) construction · production · repair removed from `AiBase`. StandardAi = squad capture · scouting · main attack · defense, PassiveAi = defense in front of the base, BattleAi = the old engagement test AI. **The default AI is the tactical AI** - see "전술 AI 1단계" (tactical AI stage 1) below.
- Save: SUPL section = per-unit Ammo · AmmoFrac · Cargo · LastFiredAtTick + BattleAmmo + trench auto-occupation (per-unit TrenchSpot · InTrench · TrenchIgnore, FormatVersion 18), checksum `HashSupply`. Load `--stress R` = per faction an ammo-0 blob + 4 wagons (`World.SupplyProf`).
